# Eunjin Grace Hong

**Researcher | Clinical NLP & Multimodal AI**

Researcher at the **Seoul National University Hospital (SNUH)** Biomedical Research Institute and the **Language Technology Research Institute (LTRI)** at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies. I hold an M.S. in AI Convergence from HUFS.


## Research Interests

My research interests follow two complementary directions:

1. **Integrating AI into real-world clinical practice** — Building and evaluating AI systems for clinical workflows, including chart review, medical documentation, and communication between healthcare providers.
2. **Developing AI methods to better address the complexity of medical contexts** — Improving reasoning, reliability, and grounding in language and multimodal models to capture clinically relevant information.
